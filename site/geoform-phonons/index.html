Model and source

01 / Reciprocal space

Dispersion

Signed cyclic frequency (THz); values below 0 indicate imaginary modes. Bands are sorted, not eigenvector-tracked.

Move a pointer across the plot to inspect the nearest q-point.

02 / Vibrational states

Phonon density of states

Gaussian-broadened states per THz per primitive cell, withheld when a sampled mode is imaginary.

Read-only model output.

03 / Atomic contributions

Atom-projected vibrational density of states

Normalized mass-weighted eigenvector projections; not experimental neutron weights. Every primitive atom contributes three modes when integrated.

Available only for stable computed spectra.

04 / Temperature dependence

Constant-volume heat capacity

Quantum harmonic heat capacity; no anharmonic temperature effects.

Available only for stable computed spectra.

05 / Equilibrium response

Harmonic thermodynamics

Per primitive cell at the exported temperature, constant volume. Not a prediction of thermal expansion or conductivity.
